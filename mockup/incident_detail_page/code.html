PORT OF SPAIN — Police are currently on the scene of a shooting incident that occurred late Tuesday night on the busy Ariapita Avenue strip in Woodbrook. Preliminary reports indicate that shortly after 10:00 PM, several loud explosions were heard in the vicinity of a popular bar.

Eyewitnesses claim a dispute broke out between two individuals before one party produced a firearm. One person has been rushed to the Port of Spain General Hospital with gunshot wounds. Officers from the Woodbrook Police Station have cordoned off a section of the avenue, causing significant traffic delays for patrons in the area.

"It was chaos, people were running for cover," said one patron who was dining nearby. "The police arrived within minutes but the shooter had already fled in a waiting vehicle heading west." Investigations are continuing. No arrests have been made at this time.
